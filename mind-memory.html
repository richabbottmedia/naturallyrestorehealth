Mind & Memory

Keeping a sharp, curious mind for the long haul.

Forgetting a name now and then is a normal part of getting older. What we do every day, from walking to learning new things to staying in touch with people, has a lot to do with how our minds age.

Everyday habits that help

  • Move your body

    Regular walking and other activity is one of the best-supported habits for brain health.

  • Keep learning

    A new language, an instrument, a craft or a course gives the brain fresh work to do.

  • Stay connected

    Regular conversation and time with friends and family matter for the mind as well as the heart.

  • Protect your sleep

    The brain does important maintenance during deep sleep.

  • Check your hearing

    Untreated hearing loss is linked with faster memory decline, so get your hearing tested.

  • See your doctor about changes

    Memory changes that worry you or your family deserve a proper check. Some causes, like low B12 or thyroid problems, are treatable.
